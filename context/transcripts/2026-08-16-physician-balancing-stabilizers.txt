Reading: the reference animations show prime movers lighting up; what she wants
added is the simultaneous counter-tension — stabilizers on the opposing side
increasing tension so the body stays balanced while the prime movers act. This
retroactively explains the posterior roster she sent on 2026-08-15: iliocostalis
cervicis / longissimus cervicis are the posterior counter-tension for the
anterior accessory inhale muscles (scalenes, SCM, pec minor).
